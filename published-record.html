Published record

Published Record

Public sources, dated records and external materials relevant to the identity, recognition and provenance of The Unicorn Card.

The subject of the record

The Unicorn Card is the 2018 Topps Future Stars FS-5 Shohei Ohtani Black Parallel.

Many collectors consider the 2018 Topps Future Stars FS-5 Shohei Ohtani Black Parallel to be the greatest Ohtani rookie card. This archive documents the evidence behind that view: the card's identity, rarity, recognition, provenance and published historical record.

Purpose

This page identifies the public sources that help establish the card’s issue identity, grading history, market record, chronology and later interpretation.

No single source establishes the entire history. The record emerges by keeping separate what each source proves, what it merely suggests and what remains unresolved.

Primary and institutional sources

Topps

2018 Topps Future Stars checklist and issue identity

Manufacturer and checklist information establish FS-5 as the Shohei Ohtani card within the 2018 Topps Future Stars release and distinguish the Black Parallel from the standard issue and other parallels.

The physical card remains the primary evidence of identity. A later grading label or database record describes the object, but it does not determine what Topps originally printed.

PSA

Population Report and certification records

PSA’s live databases provide grading-population and certification information for cards represented within PSA’s catalogue.

Population figures are snapshots of PSA’s database. They do not establish the original print run, the total surviving population or the number of examples held outside PSA holders.

A Black Parallel encapsulated under the identity of the base FS-5 card may also remain absent from the population category intended for the Black Parallel.

PSA · April 21, 2021

Acquisition of Genamint

On April 21, 2021, PSA announced its acquisition of Genamint. PSA described Genamint’s technology as capable of real-time card analysis, diagnostics, measurements, alteration detection and unique card identification in support of human graders.

The announcement is relevant to the broader history of card recognition because it documents the movement of machine-assisted analysis into PSA’s stated grading direction by 2021.

It does not establish that Genamint, PSA AI or any other specific machine-vision system examined or misidentified any particular Unicorn Card. The archive makes no such claim without direct evidence connecting the technology to a specific holder.

Market and transaction records

SportsCardsPro

FS-5 Black Parallel sales history

SportsCardsPro maintains a dedicated price-guide and sales-history page for the 2018 Topps Future Stars Shohei Ohtani Black Parallel, card FS-5.

As presently displayed, that dedicated record shows no recorded sale of the correctly identified FS-5 Black Parallel before 2024.

This absence does not prove that no earlier sale occurred. It establishes only that no pre-2024 sale presently appears in SportsCardsPro’s public record under the correctly identified Black Parallel category.

Private transactions, unindexed listings, incomplete database coverage and sales recorded under the base FS-5 identity may remain outside that record.

Archive terminology

Stealth Transactions

The archive uses the term Stealth Transaction for a sale involving an authentic FS-5 Black Parallel that was catalogued under an incorrect or incomplete identity.

The transaction may be genuine and publicly recorded while remaining absent from searches intended to locate sales of the Black Parallel.

The term describes a limitation in classification and retrieval. It does not imply deliberate concealment, deception or wrongdoing.

What the record establishes together

The surviving sources support a sequence: Topps issued the FS-5 Black Parallel in 2018; some examples passed through grading or market systems under incomplete or incorrect identities; PSA publicly announced its acquisition of Genamint in 2021; and the dedicated SportsCardsPro record for the correctly identified Black Parallel presently begins in 2024.

These facts should not be forced into a stronger causal claim. They document the difference between an object’s physical identity, the classifications assigned to it and the point at which the public record finally begins to recognize it correctly.

Archive distinctions

Documented fact
A claim supported by an object, dated record, primary source, institutional statement or verifiable database.
Database observation
A description of what a particular database presently displays. It is not automatically a complete account of every historical event.
Collector terminology
A name used to describe the card or a class of examples, but not an official manufacturer or grading-company designation.
Inference
A conclusion reasonably drawn from documented evidence but not stated directly by the underlying source.
Interpretation
A conclusion about the cultural, historical or symbolic significance of documented evidence.
Correction
A revision made when stronger evidence changes, narrows or clarifies the historical record.
