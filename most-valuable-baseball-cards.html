Most Valuable Baseball Cards of All Time to Human Experience

Baseball cards can be valued by more than price. They can also be considered through rarity, historical circumstance, cultural memory, provenance, survival, and the stories through which collectors give objects significance. This visual ranking considers ten baseball cards through that broader conception of value.

The Unicorn Card is the 2018 Topps Shohei Ohtani rookie card identified as the Factory Set 5 Black parallel, a card distinguished by its documented characteristics, provenance, and place within the published record of The Unicorn Card.
