The sequence

Obscurity. Misidentification. Recognition.

01

Obscurity

The 2018 Topps Future Stars FS-5 Shohei Ohtani Black Parallel carried no serial number announcing what it was. Its scarcity could remain unseen.

02

Misidentification

Some examples entered grading holders under labels that failed to identify the Black Parallel.

03

Recognition

Collectors began reconstructing the card's identity, graded population and historical significance.

The surviving evidence

The mistake survived.

A mislabel remains a mistake. But a 2018 Topps Future Stars FS-5 Shohei Ohtani Black Parallel still sealed inside a mislabeled holder preserves something more than an error: physical evidence that the card once passed through the modern hobby under the wrong identity.

“The mislabel is not the metaphor. The mislabel is the surviving evidence that the metaphor happened.”
